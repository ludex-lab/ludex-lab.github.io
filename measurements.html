How to read these

Where a record names a specific model or command-line tool, it is reporting one surface, one injection shape, and a small number of runs. It is not a ranking of models, and it will not support being read as one.

Pre-registration means the specification was frozen before the measurement ran, and every later change was argued in writing against people obliged to push back. For this first record the ordering rests on our working repository's history, so it is pre-registered but not independently verifiable. From the next record onward the specification is published before the run, which fixes that at no cost.
