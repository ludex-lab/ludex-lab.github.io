How to read these

Where a record names a specific model or command-line tool, it is reporting one surface, one injection shape, and a small number of runs. It is not a ranking of models, and it will not support being read as one.

Pre-registration means the specification was frozen before the measurement ran, and every later change was argued in writing against people obliged to push back. From E3 and our alphaXiv research bundles onward, every release also passes an automated cover-versus-folder manifest check so ignore rules cannot silently omit promised raw data files.
